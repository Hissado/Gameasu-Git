Please completely redesign the **Excel Export Engine** for all reports within Gaméasù to generate professional, presentation-ready, and fully dynamic Excel workbooks.

The exported Excel files must not be simple data dumps or flat tables. Instead, they should look like reports prepared by a finance department, consulting firm, auditor, or enterprise ERP system.

## Objective

Every exported Excel report must be:

* Professional
* Executive-ready
* Board-ready
* Investor-ready
* Audit-ready
* Fully formatted
* Dynamic
* Formula-driven
* Easy to analyze
* Easy to share
* Easy to update

The final workbook should be something that a CFO, CEO, Board Member, Auditor, Bank, Investor, Donor, or Department Head can immediately use without additional formatting.

# 3. Dynamic Formula-Based Reports

Do NOT export hard-coded totals.

All totals, subtotals, ratios, percentages, variances, and calculations must be generated using Excel formulas.

Examples:

* SUM()
* SUMIFS()
* COUNTIFS()
* AVERAGE()
* IF()
* IFERROR()
* VLOOKUP()
* XLOOKUP()
* INDEX()
* MATCH()
* SUBTOTAL()
* OFFSET()
* Pivot formulas

The workbook must continue calculating if users update the underlying data.
